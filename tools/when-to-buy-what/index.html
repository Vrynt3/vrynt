How this works

Car prices aren't just about the car — they're about who else wants it that month. Convertibles are cheapest when there's snow on the ground. AWD costs a premium the week before the first storm and goes soft in July when nobody's thinking about winter. Dealers get desperate at month-end, quarter-end, and especially year-end, and factories pay them to clear outgoing model years every fall. None of this is a secret inside the store — it's just rarely explained outside it.

This tool maps your state to its climate reality (salt belt, hurricane coast, desert heat, mild coast) and crosses it with the month you're buying, so the advice fits your market — not a generic national average.
