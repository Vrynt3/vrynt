About Vrynt

I work in the car business. And I genuinely love it.

Dealerships get a bad reputation online, and most of it comes from people who've never set foot behind the desk. The reality is that dealerships are pillars of their communities — they employ hundreds to thousands of people, they sponsor local sports teams and charities, they contribute to the tax base, and they keep the service infrastructure running that car owners depend on every day.

The car business is tough. Margins are thinner than people think, overhead is massive, and manufacturers are constantly pushing franchises to do more with less. On top of all that, there's a growing movement toward direct-to-consumer sales that threatens the franchise model — the same model that has kept local dealerships rooted in communities for decades.

Vrynt's car content isn't about "beating the dealer." It's about understanding how the business works so you can walk in informed, have a real conversation, and drive away feeling good about the deal — while the dealership makes enough to keep their doors open and their people employed. That's a win-win, and it's what a healthy transaction looks like.

Beyond Cars

The investing content comes from my own portfolio. I restructured my 401(k) from a high-fee target-date fund into a low-cost index fund portfolio. I set up a 529 before my kid was born. I manage my own Roth IRA. None of this required a financial advisor — just the willingness to spend a few hours learning how it works.

The crypto content comes from actively holding and managing Bitcoin. Not day-trading, not meme coins — just a structured approach to accumulating, taking profits, and managing risk through volatile cycles.

Everything on this site is written from experience. I've made these decisions, lived with the results, and now I'm sharing what I've learned.
